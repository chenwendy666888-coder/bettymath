What is number sense / 什么是数感

Key terms 关键词
number sense 数感a feel for how numbers behave
flexible 灵活many ways to see one number
estimate 估算a quick reasonable guess
from zero 从零assume nothing
IN ENGLISH

Number sense is a feel for numbers — their signs, size, distance, and how they grow — and the flexibility to see one number many ways (½ = 0.5 = 50%). It is what lets you estimate, spot a wrong answer, and calculate smartly instead of grinding. This note has 11 short blocks; read English first, then Chinese, and do the tiny example in each.

中文详解 · In Chinese

数感就是对数字的一种「感觉」——它们的符号、大小、距离、增长快慢——以及把同一个数灵活地看成好几种样子（½ = 0.5 = 50%）。有了它，你能估算、能一眼看出答案不对、能巧算而不是死算。这份有 11 个小块；先英后中，每块都做一下那道小例题。

01

Signs: + and − / 正负号与符号运算

Key terms 关键词
same sign 同号both + or both −
different signs 异号one + and one −
product 积result of ×
quotient 商result of ÷
IN ENGLISH

For × and ÷: same signs give +, different signs give −. So (−)×(−)=+, (+)×(−)=−. Why negative × negative = positive? A negative means “reverse it”; reversing a reversal points forward again. Most early slips are just a lost minus, so watch signs closely.

中文详解 · In Chinese

做 × 和 ÷：同号得正、异号得负。所以 (−)×(−)=+，(+)×(−)=−。为什么负×负=正？负号是「掉个头」，把掉头再掉一次头就又朝前了。初学的错多半只是丢了个负号，要盯紧符号。

× / ÷ 符号表 Sign table

例 Example

EN: Compute (−2)³ × (−3)², and give the sign of (−1)²⁰²⁴ × (−5).

算 (−2)³×(−3)²，并判断 (−1)²⁰²⁴×(−5) 的符号。

解: (−2)³=−8、(−3)²=9 → −8×9 = −72。(−1)²⁰²⁴=+1（偶数次幂）→ +1×(−5) = −5（负）。奇偶决定 (−1)ⁿ 的符号。

02

Absolute value = distance / 绝对值就是距离

Key terms 关键词
absolute value 绝对值|a|, always ≥ 0
distance 距离how far from a point
|a|distance of a from 0
|a − b|distance between a and b
IN ENGLISH

|a| is the distance from a to 0, so never negative: |−3|=3, |0|=0. Even better, |a−b| is the distance between a and b. Seeing it as distance (not “drop the minus”) makes |x|<3 obvious: it means “within 3 of 0”, i.e. −3<x<3.

中文详解 · In Chinese

|a| 是 a 到 0 的距离，所以永不为负：|−3|=3、|0|=0。更妙的是 |a−b| 是 a 与 b 的距离。把它当距离（而非「去掉负号」），|x|<3 就很显然：「离 0 不超过 3」，即 −3<x<3。

例 Example

EN: 【同型·数轴】Find the smallest value of |x−1| + |x−5|.

【同型·数轴】求 |x−1|+|x−5| 的最小值。

解: 读成「到 1 的距离 + 到 5 的距离」。当 x 落在 1 与 5 之间，两段之和恒等于 1 到 5 的距离 = 4（即最小值）。数形结合秒解。

03

Number line & ordering / 数轴与大小

Key terms 关键词
number line 数轴numbers left→right
negative 负数left of 0
greater 更大further right
compare 比较which is bigger
IN ENGLISH

On the number line, further right = bigger. So −5 < −2 (−5 is further left). Trap: with negatives, “bigger-looking digits” can mean a smaller number — −9 < −1. When unsure, picture the line.

中文详解 · In Chinese

数轴上越往右越大。所以 −5 < −2（−5 更靠左）。坑：负数里「数字看着大」反而更小——−9 < −1。拿不准就脑补一条数轴。

例 Example

EN: Put in order (small→large): −32, −√2, −1.4.

从小到大排：−32、−√2、−1.4。

解: 化小数：−1.5、−1.414…、−1.4。越靠左越小 → −32 < −√2 < −1.4。负数里绝对值大反而小。

04

Fractions · decimals · percents / 三者互通

Key terms 关键词
fraction 分数½, ¾ …
decimal 小数0.5, 0.75 …
percent 百分数50%, 75% …
equivalent 等值same value
IN ENGLISH

½, 0.5 and 50% are the same number in different clothes. Top ÷ bottom gives the decimal (¾ = 3÷4 = 0.75); ×100 gives percent. Knowing ½=0.5, ¼=0.25, ⅓≈0.33, ⅕=0.2 by heart gives instant size sense.

中文详解 · In Chinese

½、0.5、50% 是同一个数换了衣服。分子÷分母得小数（¾=0.75），×100 得百分数。记住 ½=0.5、¼=0.25、⅓≈0.33、⅕=0.2，几乎每题都能立刻有大小感。

例 Example

EN: Which is largest: 37, 0.43, 44%?

谁最大：37、0.43、44%？

解: 37≈0.4286、0.43、0.44 → 44% 最大、37 最小。全化成小数再比。

05

Powers & roots, intuitively / 幂与根的直觉

Key terms 关键词
power 幂aⁿ = a×…×a
square/cube 平方/立方a², a³
square root 平方根√ undoes squaring
grows fast 增长快squares blow up
IN ENGLISH

A power is repeated multiplying: 2³=8. Squares grow fast (1,4,9,16,25…). A root undoes a power: √16=4. Surprise sense: the root of a number between 0 and 1 gets bigger (√0.25 = 0.5).

中文详解 · In Chinese

幂是重复相乘：2³=8。平方涨得快（1,4,9,16,25…）。根是幂的逆：√16=4。反直觉：0~1 之间开根会变大（√0.25=0.5）。

例 Example

EN: Compute √0.0016 ; is 3⁷ closer to 2,000 or 20,000?

算 √0.0016；3⁷ 更接近 2000 还是 20000？

解: √0.0016 = 0.04（0.04²=0.0016）。3⁷=2187 → 更接近 2000。幂涨得快，但要有数量级感。

06

Estimate first & benchmarks / 估算与凑整

Key terms 关键词
estimate 估算a quick reasonable value
round 取整to a friendly number
benchmark 基准数½, ¼, 10, 100
sanity check 合理性does it look right?
IN ENGLISH

Before you compute, estimate by rounding to friendly numbers — it catches big blunders. 43×7 ≈ 40×7 = 280, so an answer of 201 must be wrong. Benchmarks like ½, ¼, 10, 100 give instant size sense; always anchor with an estimate first.

中文详解 · In Chinese

算之前先估一个——用凑整，能抓大错。43×7 ≈ 40×7 = 280，所以答 201 一定错。½、¼、整十整百这些基准数给你即时大小感；养成「先估再算」的习惯。

例 Example

EN: 【同型·凑整】Compute 39 × 41 fast; estimate √50 to the nearest integer.

【同型】快算 39×41；把 √50 估到最近整数。

解: 39×41=(40−1)(40+1)=40²−1 = 1599。7²=49、8²=64 → √50 ≈ 7。

07

Decompose & compensate / 拆分与补偿

Key terms 关键词
decompose 拆分split by place value
compensate 补偿borrow to a round number
friendly number 顺手数10, 100, 50 …
recombine 重组put back together
IN ENGLISH

Break numbers into place-value chunks: 47+38 = (40+30)+(7+8) = 85. Or compensate to a round number: 59+32 → 60+31 = 91; 98×6 = 100×6 − 2×6 = 588. Round numbers are far easier for your brain, so nudge toward them.

中文详解 · In Chinese

把数按位值拆开：47+38=(40+30)+(7+8)=85。或补偿成整数：59+32→60+31=91；98×6=100×6−2×6=588。整数最好算，尽量往整数靠。

例 Example

EN: Compute 103 × 97 and 998 × 1002 without a calculator.

不用计算器算 103×97、998×1002。

解: 103×97=(100+3)(100−3)=100²−9 = 9991；998×1002=1000²−4 = 999996。平方差凑整。

08

Doubling & halving / 倍半法

Key terms 关键词
double 加倍×2
halve 减半÷2
friendly 顺手数5,10,50,100
balance 平衡product unchanged
IN ENGLISH

Halve one factor and double the other until one becomes friendly — the product stays the same: 16×25 = 8×50 = 4×100 = 400. Perfect for ×5, ×25, ×50. Rule of thumb: halve until you hit 5、10、50、100, double the other to match.

中文详解 · In Chinese

一个减半、另一个加倍，直到出现顺手数——积不变：16×25 = 8×50 = 4×100 = 400。×5、×25、×50 特别好用。诀窍：减半到 5、10、50、100，另一个相应加倍。

例 Example

EN: Compute 125 × 48 by doubling & halving.

用倍半法算 125×48。

解: 125×48 = 125×8×6 = 1000×6 = 6000（从 48 拆出 8 凑成 1000）。

Factors, multiples & divisibility / 因数·倍数·整除

Key terms 关键词
factor 因数divides it exactly
multiple 倍数a whole number of times
divisible 整除no remainder
digit sum 数字和add the digits
IN ENGLISH

Divisibility rules let you check without dividing: ÷2 if it ends even; ÷5 if it ends 0 or 5; ÷10 if it ends 0; ÷3 if the digit-sum is a multiple of 3; ÷9 if the digit-sum is a multiple of 9; ÷4 if the last two digits are a multiple of 4. Great for simplifying fractions fast.

中文详解 · In Chinese

整除判定不用真的去除：末位偶数→÷2；末位 0 或 5→÷5；末位 0→÷10；数字和是 3 的倍数→÷3；数字和是 9 的倍数→÷9；末两位是 4 的倍数→÷4。化简分数飞快。

例 Example

EN: 【同型·平和】How many of 1..100 are multiples of 6? What is the chance a random card is one?

【同型·平和】1~100 中 6 的倍数有几个？随机抽一张抽到的概率？

解: 6,12,…,96 共 16 个（⌊1006⌋）。概率 = 16100 = 425。整除 + 计数。

Place value & ×10ⁿ / 位值与 ×10ⁿ

Key terms 关键词
place value 位值each place is ×10 of the next
×10 左移shift up one place
÷10 右移shift down one place
power of ten 10 的幂×100 = ×10²
IN ENGLISH

Each place is 10 times the one to its right. ×10 moves the decimal one place right (2.5→25), ÷10 one place left (2.5→0.25). ×100 = ×10², ×1000 = ×10³. This is the seed of scientific notation and makes big/small numbers easy.

中文详解 · In Chinese

每一位是右边一位的 10 倍。×10 把小数点右移一位（2.5→25），÷10 左移一位（2.5→0.25）。×100=×10²、×1000=×10³。这就是科学计数法的种子，处理大数小数很省事。

例 Example

EN: Write 34,500 as a×10ⁿ (1≤a<10); compute 0.0025 × 10⁴.

把 34500 写成 a×10ⁿ（1≤a<10）；算 0.0025×10⁴。

解: 34500 = 3.45×10⁴（科学计数法）；0.0025×10⁴ = 25（小数点右移 4 位）。

11

Ratio & proportion sense / 比与比例的直觉

Key terms 关键词
ratio 比a : b
proportion 比例two equal ratios
scale 缩放grow/shrink together
unit value 单位量cost/amount per one
IN ENGLISH

Ratios scale together: since 5 is half of 10, 50 is half of 100. A reliable move is to find the “per one” (unit) value, then scale: 3 pens cost 12 → 1 costs 4 → 5 cost 20. Proportional thinking underlies percents, speed, and maps.

中文详解 · In Chinese

比例是一起缩放的：5 是 10 的一半，50 也是 100 的一半。稳妥办法是先求「一个多少」（单位量）再放大：3 支笔 12 元 → 1 支 4 元 → 5 支 20 元。百分数、速度、比例尺背后都是它。

例 Example

EN: 【同型·比例】If a:b = 2:3 and b:c = 4:5, find a:c. An alloy is copper:zinc = 3:2; in 40 kg, how much copper?

【同型】若 a:b=2:3、b:c=4:5，求 a:c；铜锌 3:2 的 40 kg 合金含铜多少？

解: 统一 b：a:b=8:12、b:c=12:15 → a:b:c=8:12:15 → a:c=8:15。铜占 35 → 40×35 = 24 kg。
